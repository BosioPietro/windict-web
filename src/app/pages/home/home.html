Look up

Definitions, in any app

Browsers, PDFs, e-mail, code editors: the card opens right under the word, and or a click elsewhere closes it.

More about looking up words

  • Offline, and instant

    Over 150,000 English words and phrases from Open English WordNet are built in, so no connection is needed.

  • Every sense, with examples

    Senses are grouped by part of speech, with example sentences and similar and opposite words a click away.

  • In Italian too

    About 67,000 Italian words from Wikizionario, and an online dictionary for the words neither has.

Translate

Translate as you read

Select a word, a phrase or a whole passage and press . On a definition, translates the word.

More about translating

  • 22 languages

    From English and Italian to Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

  • Languages picked for you

    WinDict tells the language from its words, and you can pick or swap them on the card.

  • Whole passages

    Up to 500 characters at once, or type the text into a box when it can't be selected.

Above Start

Right on top of Start and Search

Windows keeps Start and Search above every other window. Installed with its setup, WinDict can show the card on top of them too, so you can look up a word without leaving the Start menu.

How it works

Small, with everything you need

Written in Rust against the native Windows APIs, WinDict feels like part of Windows and costs next to nothing while it waits.

Ready in a minute

  1. 1

    Download the setup

    From the latest release on GitHub, or with WinGet:

  2. 2

    Run it

    It installs WinDict for everyone on the PC and adds it to Start. A short introduction shows you around.

  3. 3

    Select a word, press Alt+D

    WinDict waits in the notification area, by the clock. Click its icon for the settings.
