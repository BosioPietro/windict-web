Look up

Definitions in place, in any app

Browsers, PDFs, e-mail, code editors: select a word or a short phrase, press , and the card opens right under it. or a click elsewhere closes it.

  • About 150,000 English words and phrases built in, from Open English WordNet. No connection needed.
  • Senses grouped by part of speech, each with an example sentence, and similar and opposite words a click away.
  • Italian too, with about 75,000 words from Wikizionario, and the online dictionary for words neither has.

Translate

Translate as you read

Select a word, a phrase or a whole passage of up to 500 characters and press : the card opens on its translation. On a definition, translates the word.

  • 22 languages, from English and Italian to Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
  • Languages picked for you. WinDict tells the language from its words, and the card lets you pick or swap them.
More about translating

Above Start

Right on top of Start and Search

Windows keeps Start and Search above every other window. Installed with its setup, WinDict can show the card on top of them too, so you can look up a word without leaving the Start menu.

How it works

Small, with everything you need

Written in Rust against the native Windows APIs, WinDict feels like part of Windows and costs next to nothing while it waits.

Get started

Up and running in a minute

  1. 1

    Download the setup

    Get WinDict-<version>-setup.exe from the latest release on GitHub.

  2. 2

    Run it

    It installs WinDict for everyone on the PC and adds it to Start. A short introduction shows you around.

  3. 3

    Select a word, press Alt+D

    WinDict waits in the notification area, by the clock. Click its icon for the settings.
