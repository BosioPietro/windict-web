Keyboard shortcuts

Every key WinDict listens for, from anywhere in Windows and on the card.

Anywhere in Windows

These two work in every app. To change them, open Shortcuts under General in the settings, click the one to change and press the new keys; keeps the old ones. A shortcut needs , or with its key.

Keys What they do
Looks up the selected word or phrase. With nothing selected, opens a box to type a word into.
Translates the selected text, up to 500 characters. With nothing selected, opens a box to type text into.

On the card

While a card is open, these keys act on it, without having to click it first.

Keys What they do
Closes the card
Shows the word's translation, or its definition again
Copies the text selected, or else the word, the translation, or the similar word picked with Tab
On the translation of a selection, types it over the selection and closes the card
Goes back to the word before. The mouse's back button does too.
Moves between the card's links and buttons
Opens the link or presses the button picked with Tab
Scrolls the card
Makes the text larger or smaller. So does Ctrl with the mouse wheel.
Goes back to the usual text size

On a pinned card

A pinned card takes the keyboard once you click it, like any other window. The keys above work there too, and:

Keys What they do
Closes the pinned card
Closes it, as it closes any window

In the box to type into

Keys What they do
Looks up the word, or translates the text
Picks one of the suggestions under the box
Picks the next or the previous suggestion, or the button that reads the screen
Pastes
Selects all the text, for what's typed next to replace
Moves the caret, by words with Ctrl, selecting what it passes with Shift
Moves the caret to the start or the end, selecting up to there with Shift
Deletes what's selected, or before or after the caret, a word at a time with Ctrl
Closes the box

In the list of languages

When you click a language on a translation, type to filter the list.

Keys What they do
Moves through the languages, as Tab and Shift+Tab do
Moves a page at a time
Picks the language
Closes the list

In the tray menu

Keys What they do
Moves through the menu
Goes to the first or the last item
